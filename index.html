Research begins before the mission does.

Dumper Space Research Organisation is built around questions, not launches. Space agencies carry funded, scheduled work, and they should. That work leaves gaps: worlds visited once, results people still argue about, ideas that are too early for any roadmap.

We work in those gaps. We are not here to compete with NASA, ISRO, ESA or SpaceX. We are here to ask what they have not yet had room to ask.

We would rather be known for one idea that holds up than for a hundred announcements.

Every major breakthrough begins with a question. The future belongs to those willing to ask the next one.
